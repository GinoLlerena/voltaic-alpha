What the model never sees

An absence cannot be bypassed.

The entire model output schema

RESPONSE_SCHEMA — strict: true
direction              bullish|bearish|neutral
confidence             0 … 1
evidence_ids           string[]
counter_evidence_ids   string[]
reasoning_summary      ≤ 900 chars

additionalProperties: false

Absent by construction

  • Invalidation. No field exists. It is set from the deterministic classifier, and never shown to the model.
  • Sizing, contracts, eligibility. Not in the schema, not in the prompt.
  • Direction is stated but not owned. A reversal against the setup is coerced to neutral and recorded as model_attempted_direction_reversal.

A check can be deleted in a refactor while every test still passes. A field that does not exist in a strict schema cannot be emitted at all.

Hash lineage

Every order chains back to the observation that justified it.

decision hash   →   intent hash   →   request hash   →   broker order

client_order_id = f"{CLIENT_ID_PREFIX}-{digest[:28]}"
                  the first 28 hex of the intent hash
  • Idempotency is derived, not generated. A duplicate submit collides at the broker instead of opening a second position.
  • Ambiguous writes recover by lookup, not retry — re-submitting is how duplicates are created.
  • Broker acknowledgement is SUBMITTED, never FILLED. A fill is only ever written from a reconciled broker record.

Business case

Who buys an audit layer.

The buyer

  • Teams shipping agentic trading applications who must answer a compliance or risk reviewer.
  • The buyer is anyone who must explain an automated decision to someone with authority over them.

Why it is worth money

  • Reconstruction is cheaper to build in than to retrofit — a hash chain cannot be added to decisions already made.
  • Broker- and strategy-agnostic by construction: the gateway, synthesizer and execution port are protocols. SPY verticals are the demonstration, not the product.

Limits

Disclosures.

01

Alpaca Paper only. No live endpoint exists in this build, and the gateway refuses any resolved endpoint that is not the Paper host — checked against the client about to be used, not the flag that configured it.

02

Option quotes come from the indicative feed. The account has no OPRA agreement, so quotes are not trading-quality. On 31 August 2026 that feed showed a contract 8 cents below where it was trading, which is enough to make an order unfillable. Prices here should not be read as executable marks.

03

No alpha is claimed. The null hypothesis — that this setup has no advantage over buying and holding SPY after costs — is the project's default assumption and is not rejected. The sample is two trades. Any P&L shown is a diagnostic reported with its sample size, never evidence of an edge.

04

Nothing here is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer. It is an engineering demonstration of an execution boundary.
